# TF2 Farm

![TF2 Farm](images/16_tf2_farm.png)

TF2 item farming

1. **Add accounts**
2. Accounts play in 5-minute sessions: an item dropped — done, no item — back to the end of the queue
3. Items are accepted by themselves, you see what dropped and an estimated USD price

Threads — shared, in the header · **Stop** — end the sessions
