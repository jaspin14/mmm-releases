# Craft Skins

![Craft Skins](images/12_craft.png)

CS2 contract: **10 items of one rarity → 1 of the next**

1. **+ Add** → **Load inventory**
2. **Craft selected** or **Craft all**

**Smart craft** — builds sets with odds and prices by itself. **Profitable only** — where the expected price is not below the cost
